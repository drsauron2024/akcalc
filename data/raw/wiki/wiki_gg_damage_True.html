True damage

From Arknights Terra Wiki
< Damage
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Damage
Physical Arts True Elemental

True damage, formerly referred to as Pure damage, is not reduced by DEF or RES and ignores the majority of damage mitigation effects, thus it will normally always be inflicted at exactly the specified amount. Only damage mitigations that do not specify a damage type work against True damage such as Kristen Wright's trait.

In the game files, True damage is defined as a distinct damage type that is not subject to modifiers toward the other damage types, which distinguishes it from damage that ignores defenses (as they would still be of a certain type nonetheless, such as the Physical damage from Franka's Thermite Blade talent).

Before an update, True damage was defined in-game as "damage that ignores DEF and RES".

Other

  • The vast majority of HP drain/loss effects, such as Active Originium and Poison Haze, are treated as True damage.
  • The eruption of Heat Pump Passages and Lava Cracks deals True damage.
  • Burning Breath deals True damage over time that increases over its duration.
  • Nervous Impairment deals 1000 True damage when the burst effect is triggered.
  • The High-Energy Originium Bomb and Interference Mine deal True damage to enemies in range upon detonation.
  • Enemies hit by a collapsing Tattered Pillar receive True damage equal to the Pillar's ATK.
  • Originium Altars periodically deal 500 True damage to both friendly units and enemies (except Sarkaz Guerrilla Fighters and Sarkaz Guerrilla Casters, Mercenary Sarkaz Fighters and Mercenary Sarkaz Casters, and Patriot) in a radius of 2 tiles around them.
    • Patriot's Originium Altar in R8-6 deals 2000 True damage to enemy units instead.
  • The Londinium Secondary Defense Artillery deals True damage to all friendly units and enemies within range.
  • Pulse Defense Modules deal 5000 True damage to enemy units in a radius of 3 tiles around their lower part when activated.
  • In Integrated Strategies:
    • (Phantom & Crimson Solitaire) When the "Survivalist" Hallucination is active, friendly units and enemies will take 50 and 100 True damage, respectively, every second.
    • The "Excessiveness" (Crimson Solitaire) and "Memories of The Shard" (Mizuki & Caerula Arbor onwards) Collectibles cause all enemies present to receive 3000 True damage whenever an enemy enters a Protection Objective.
    • Spiked Treasure Chests reflect 40% of damage taken as True damage.
  • In Stationary Security Service:
    • If a deployed Contaminated Mineral is not destroyed in time, it will explode, dealing 1000 True damage to friendly units caught in its blast.
    • Whenever a Heat Cluster is transferred into the Deployment Waiting Zone, all friendly units will receive 500 True damage.
    • Choosing the High-Efficiency Baking Powder in Bottomless Pit will cause all friendlies to receive 15 True damage per second for each Powder taken.
  • In Reclamation Algorithm:
    • During the Thunderburst and Thunderstorm weathers, lightning strikes periodically deal 800 True damage to all units in range.
      • Lightning strikes in other modes such as Rhodes Island Icebreaker Games and Integrated Strategies have variable amounts of damage.
    • Operators that have consumed Distilled Teardrop Ointment deal an extra 3% of their ATK as True damage whenever dealing Physical damage.

Interactions

The "Civilight Eterna" collectible in Integrated Strategies increases the True damage taken by enemies by 150%.
